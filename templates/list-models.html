Model Data — Optional

Add your models — pick one way

There are three ways to get your roster in. Use whichever is easiest — you only need one. You can always review and edit everything afterwards.

Method 1 — Compcards (easiest)

Upload your models' compcard images. We read each card's name and measurements automatically — no typing.

Method 2 — Spreadsheet + Photos

Upload a CSV of measurements and a ZIP of photos together (photos matched to rows by name).

↓ Download CSV template (model_template.csv)
Name each photo to match the model's name, e.g. kim_jae_young.jpg

Template columns: name, korean_name, birth_year, gender, nationality, height, chest, waist, hips, shoes, hair, eyes, rate_usd.
Measurements can be typed in cm or inches — e.g. 186, 5'9, 28in, 99/39. Shoes accept mm (280) or EU (42eu). We normalize everything automatically.

Method 3 — From your website

Skip the uploads above and we'll import your roster from the website URL you entered. Either way, our system keeps everything up to date over time.
